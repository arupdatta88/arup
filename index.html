About

Results-driven, and results-documented.

I'm a Systems Engineer and Technical Project Coordinator with over a decade of experience spanning network engineering, IT operations, and end-to-end IT/ITES project delivery. Most of that time has been spent doing two things at once: keeping infrastructure reliable, and keeping the humans around that infrastructure — clients, developers, and stakeholders — on the same page.

I'm comfortable being the primary point of contact on a project: gathering requirements, writing the documentation nobody wants to write, running the incident call at 2am, and translating what happened into language a non-technical stakeholder can act on. I care about things finishing on time, in scope, and explained clearly afterward.

"At 38, I've learned that the best systems — technical or otherwise — are the ones you document well enough that someone else can trust them." Off the clock, I'm a husband and father of one, based in Chattogram, Bangladesh, usually with a notebook of story ideas nearby.
